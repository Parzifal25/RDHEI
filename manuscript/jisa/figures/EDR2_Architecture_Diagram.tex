\caption{Detailed EDR2 architecture. The content owner selects a step-2 phase and reserves room before AES-256-CTR image encryption. The independent hider authenticates the bootstrap, embeds an encrypted payload in the reserved ciphertext-domain slots and updates the final HMAC. The receiver authenticates the in-band phase header before directory decompression or CNN inference, then extracts the payload and reconstructs the original grayscale host.}
